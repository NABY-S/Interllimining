About us

Mining roots. A modern vault.

Interlli Mining is a gold and mining company at home in Lichtenberg, in the Free State of Bavaria, Germany.

Our vault service rests on one idea. People should be able to own real gold, know exactly where it is, and check it whenever they like.

Every account holds physical bars and coins stored in Germany and recorded by serial number. You can buy once or save a little each month, and you can see the whole picture from your phone.

Own the metal

Your gold is physical. Each bar is recorded under your name with its serial number and weight.

Know where it is

Everything sits in one place, at Amsinckstrasse 54 in Lichtenberg, Bavaria. Not abroad, not pooled.

Begin with a few grams or a monthly plan, and add more whenever you like.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is it real, physical gold?

Yes. Accounts hold physical bars and coins at the vault in Lichtenberg. Each bar carries a serial number that is recorded under your account.

What does it cost?

You pay the gold price of the day plus a service fee of 1,5 % on purchases. The converter above shows the total before you order. Storage terms are set out in the account agreement.

Do I pay tax on gold in Germany?

Investment gold is generally exempt from VAT in Germany. Private individuals who sell physical gold after holding it for more than one year generally pay no capital gains tax. Rules change and depend on your situation, so ask a tax adviser.

How much do I need to start?

From €25, either as a single purchase or as a monthly plan.

Do you check my identity?

For larger purchases, German anti-money-laundering rules require us to verify who you are. We tell you before you order.

Can I visit, collect or sell my gold?

You can arrange a visit by appointment, and we can buy your gold back at the day's price. Contact us to set either up.

Can the value go down?

Yes. The price of gold rises and falls, and what you can sell for may be less than you paid. Save only what you can leave untouched for the long term.
